Our story

In the wake of the 2020 COVID-19 pandemic, many martial arts schools had to adjust to new mandates, while others lost students and closed their doors. No Limits Taekwondo began at the end of 2020 at the request of Master William's former students from the school where he taught alongside his master, Master Park.

What started with three students has grown to ten, and the school keeps welcoming new ones. Are you ready to reach your potential?
